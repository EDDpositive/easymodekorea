How much in Seoul?

Five real prices a day, about a minute. No sign-up, no ads.

How it works

  1. Every day brings five new questions: getting around, food, an outing or a service, the market, and one bill that adds several prices together.
  2. Lay down notes and coins for what you think it costs. You get one guess for each.
  3. Points show how close you came. For something that costs ₩10,000, paying ₩5,000 gives 50 points, and so does paying ₩20,000. ▲ means you paid too much, ▼ too little, ✓ very close.
  4. Some things in Seoul are free. If you think so, pay nothing.

Questions

Where do the prices come from?

Only from official pages that state the number: Korea's national price survey (price.go.kr), the KAMIS farm and fish price service, Seoul City, the royal palaces, museums and the post office. Every answer shows its source and date.

How old are the prices?

Restaurant and service prices are Seoul averages for October 2026, which the survey marks as provisional. Market prices are Seoul shop averages for 24 November 2026. Fares and tickets were read on the official page, most of them on 25 November 2026.

Will I pay exactly this in Seoul?

Fares and tickets, yes, until they change. Meals, haircuts and groceries are averages: a place next to a famous sight often costs more, a neighbourhood place less.

Is a bowl of noodles really over ₩10,000?

On average, yes. In October 2026 the Seoul average was ₩12,692 for naengmyeon and ₩10,038 for kalguksu, while jajangmyeon was ₩7,808.

Do I need an account?

No. Your scores stay on this device and your guesses are not sent anywhere. The page only counts visits and a few button presses, without cookies.

When do the prices change?

At midnight on your device's clock. Everyone gets the same five on the same date, so you can compare with friends.

Can I play without a connection?

Yes, once you have opened the page with a connection. Older days are open too, below your result.

Which notes and coins does Korea use?

Notes are ₩50,000, ₩10,000, ₩5,000 and ₩1,000. The coins you meet most are ₩500 and ₩100. The colours in the game follow the real ones: yellow, green, red and blue.
