Step by step, first time
  1. Install Naver Map from the App Store or Google Play (listed as NAVER Map). Allow location access when asked.
  2. Switch it to English: open the menu in the app, then Settings, then Language. On iPhone you can also pick the language in the iPhone Settings app, under NAVER Map.
  3. Search for a place in English or Korean. If English finds nothing, paste the Korean name or address from your booking.
  4. Tap Directions and choose the public transport tab. Routes show the subway line, bus numbers, transfer points, the station exit number and live arrival times.
  5. At the station, follow the exit number from the route. Big stations have many exits, and the right one can save a long walk.
  6. Optional: sign in with a Naver account to save places. Visitors can verify a Naver account with a passport to book restaurants on Naver Map (since June 2026).
  7. Keep Kakao Map as a second map. Use Google Maps for finding places, not for walking or driving directions.
Common problems and fixes

  • The app stays in Korean.

    Change Language in the app's Settings. On iPhone, if NAVER Map shows no language option in iPhone Settings, add English under General, Language & Region first.

  • An English search finds nothing.

    Paste the Korean name from your booking, or search for the nearest station and walk from there.

  • The route looks very long or goes by car.

    Check that you picked the public transport or walking tab, not the car tab.

  • Saving or booking asks you to log in.

    Directions work without an account. Saving places needs a Naver account, and booking needs a one-time passport check in the app.

Questions people ask

Does Google Maps work in Korea?

It finds places and gives public transport routes, but walking and driving directions are limited. The government conditionally approved Google's map-data export in February 2026; better Google routing had not arrived at our last check.

Naver Map or Kakao Map: which is better?

Both give full transit and walking directions. Naver Map has English, Japanese and Chinese menus and passport-verified bookings, so start there and keep Kakao Map as a second opinion.

Do I need a Korean phone number to use Naver Map?

No. Search and directions work without an account. You only need an account to save places, write reviews or book.

Does Naver Map work without mobile data?

Plan on needing data. Check your route on Wi-Fi first and take a screenshot of the steps if your signal is patchy.
