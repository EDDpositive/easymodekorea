Step by step, first time
  1. Before you fly, install k.ride (by Kakao Mobility) or Uber and sign up on home Wi-Fi. k.ride takes an email, Google or Apple account, so no Korean number is needed.
  2. Add a payment card in the app and confirm it is accepted before you land. If it fails, try another card at home rather than at the curb.
  3. Set the destination. English names work for big places. For a guesthouse or a small street, paste the Korean address from Naver Map or your host's address card.
  4. Check the pickup pin. At airports and big stations, walk to the taxi area and move the pin there so the driver can find you.
  5. When the car arrives, check that the plate number matches the one in the app before you get in.
  6. Paying: app taxis charge the card saved in the app. Street taxis take cards and T-money at the reader next to the meter, and cash.
  7. Fares in Seoul at our last check: a standard taxi starts at 4,800 won. A late-night surcharge applies from 22:00 to 04:00 (20%, and 40% from 23:00 to 02:00). Leaving Seoul adds 20%. Black deluxe taxis cost more.
Common problems and fixes

  • Your card won't register in Kakao T.

    Use k.ride, Uber or TABA, which are built for visitors, or choose pay-to-driver in Kakao T.

  • No driver accepts the call, often late at night or in rain.

    Wait a few minutes and call again, try a larger or deluxe class, or walk to a taxi stand. A hotel front desk can also call one.

  • The app can't find your address.

    Paste the Korean road-name address, or drop the pin on the building itself.

  • The driver doesn't speak English.

    The destination and payment are already in the app. Show the Korean address card or the 🗣 phrases on this page if you need to say more.

  • A street taxi's reader rejects your card.

    Pay with T-money or cash, or use an app taxi next time so the fare goes on the card saved in the app.

Questions people ask

Does Uber work in Korea?

Yes. Uber works in Seoul and other big cities with your existing account and sends regular metered taxis.

Do I tip taxi drivers in Korea?

No. Tipping is not expected in Korean taxis.

Can I hail a taxi on the street?

Yes. A lit sign on the roof or windscreen means the taxi is free. In busy areas at night, an app is usually faster than hailing.

Can I take a taxi from Incheon Airport to Seoul?

Yes, from the taxi stand outside arrivals or by app. It costs far more than the AREX train, and tolls are added to the fare.
