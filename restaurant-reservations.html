Step by step, first time
  1. Install CatchTable Global (listed as CATCH TABLE) and sign up with your email. No Korean number is needed.
  2. Search for the restaurant, choose the date, time and party size, and see whether it takes bookings or a remote waitlist.
  3. Read the cancellation rules before you pay a deposit. Each restaurant sets its own, and the app shows how much comes back if you cancel.
  4. Pay the deposit with a card from home. Waitlists usually don't need a deposit.
  5. Arrive on time. Late arrivals and no-shows can lose the booking and the deposit.
  6. For restaurants on Naver Map: verify your Naver account with your passport once (since June 2026), then book on the restaurant's page in Naver Map.
  7. Walking in? Many busy places have a waitlist tablet at the door. If it asks for a Korean number, show staff your CatchTable waitlist or ask them to add you.
Common problems and fixes

  • The restaurant isn't on CatchTable Global.

    Check Naver Map, or go early and join the line in person.

  • The waitlist tablet wants a Korean number.

    Ask staff to add you, or join the restaurant's remote waitlist in CatchTable Global if it has one.

  • Your deposit card is declined.

    Try another card. Some banks hold a first payment to a Korean merchant until you approve it in their app.

  • You need to cancel.

    Cancel in the app as early as you can. The app shows the refund before you confirm, and a cancellation can't be undone.

Questions people ask

Is the deposit refundable?

It depends on the restaurant and how early you cancel. The app shows the refund amount before you confirm a cancellation.

Do I need a Korean phone number?

No. CatchTable Global takes an email sign-up, and Naver accepts passport verification.

How far ahead should I book popular places?

As early as the restaurant opens its slots. Some popular places fill within minutes of opening.

What if a place takes no bookings at all?

Go before opening time and queue. Write the restaurant's Korean name down to show staff.
