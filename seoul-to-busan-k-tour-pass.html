How do I get from Seoul to Busan, and what is the K-Tour Pass?

Good to go

Good to go

Take the KTX and buy the K-Tour Pass pilot online first. It gives free entry to one Busan attraction and discounts on the way there, until 31 Dec 2026.

Time
KTX Seoul to Busan: about 2.5 hours ✓ 17 Nov 2026
Good to know

Step by step, first time

  1. Book your KTX to Busan on KORAIL's English site.

    Tickets open two months ahead, so book early for weekends and holidays.

  2. Buy the K-Tour Pass online before you go.

    It costs 15,000 won and is sold through travel sites such as Klook. It includes free entry to one of about 35 Busan attractions.

  3. The pass also carries discount coupons for transport to Busan, reported as 40% off rail.

    Read the coupon terms before you book.

If that failsThe pass is a pilot that runs only to 31 Dec 2026. If it is sold out or has ended, a normal KTX ticket still gets you there.

Step by step, first time
  1. Book your KTX to Busan on KORAIL's English site. Tickets open two months ahead; Friday and Sunday trains sell out first.
  2. Before you go, buy the K-Tour Pass pilot online. It costs 15,000 won and includes free entry to one of about 35 Busan attractions.
  3. Read the pass's discount coupon terms for transport to Busan before you book your train, so the discount applies.
  4. In Busan, the same T-money card works on the subway and buses.
  5. Pick your free attraction early in the trip; check the opening days on the pass page.
  6. Going back: book the return KTX at the same time as the outbound, especially on Sundays.
Common problems and fixes

  • The pass is sold out or the sales page has closed.

    The pilot runs only to 31 Dec 2026. A normal KTX ticket still gets you there.

  • The rail discount didn't apply.

    Coupons have their own terms and booking channels. Read them before buying the ticket; a ticket bought another way may not qualify.

  • Your KTX date is full.

    Try trains from Suseo or Yongsan as well as Seoul Station, or an earlier or later train.

  • An attraction won't accept the pass.

    Check the list of included attractions and opening days on the pass page.

Questions people ask

How long is the KTX from Seoul to Busan?

About 2.5 hours on the fastest trains.

Is the K-Tour Pass only for foreigners?

Yes, it is a pilot for foreign visitors run by the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism and the Korea Tourism Organization.

Is the KR Pass better than single tickets for Busan?

Only if you add more long trips. See our KR Pass guide.

Where do I store bags on a day trip?

Lockers at Busan Station, or leave them at your Seoul accommodation. See our luggage guide.
