Is the KR Pass (Korail pass) worth it?

One extra step

One extra step

The KR Pass can save money on several long rides, but you still book each seat. Add up your single fares before you buy.

Good to know
Covers KORAIL trains, not AREX or the subway ✓ 17 Nov 2026
Where
You still book each seat after buying the pass ✓ 17 Nov 2026

Checked against KORAIL English booking site on 17 Nov 2026

Step by step, first time

  1. Check you qualify. The pass is for foreign visitors, so bring your passport. Koreans and long-stay residents are not eligible.
  2. List your trips and look up each single fare on KORAIL's English site. Buy the pass only if the total is clearly higher.
  3. After buying, reserve a seat for every trip. The pass is not a seat, and popular routes sell out.

If that failsNot sure it pays off? Single KTX tickets from KORAIL's English site always work.

Check your own setup
Step by step, first time
  1. List every long train trip in your plan, with dates.
  2. Look up each single fare on KORAIL's English site and add them up.
  3. Compare that total with the KR Pass price for the number of days you need. Buy the pass only if it clearly saves money.
  4. Buy the pass online from KORAIL or an authorised seller with your passport details. It is for foreign visitors only.
  5. Reserve a seat for every trip as soon as you can. The pass does not hold a seat, and popular trains fill up.
  6. Carry your passport on board with the pass; conductors may check both.
Common problems and fixes

  • Seats are gone on your train.

    Try another departure time or station. Standing places may be allowed when seats sell out.

  • The pass won't cover a train you want.

    The pass covers KORAIL trains. It doesn't cover the AREX airport train or the subway; check the route before you buy.

  • The pass works out more expensive.

    Buy single tickets instead. They always work.

  • You need to cancel the pass.

    Refund deadlines depend on where you bought it; check the seller's terms before buying.

Questions people ask

Who can buy the KR Pass?

Foreign visitors with a passport. Koreans and long-stay residents are not eligible.

Does the KR Pass include seat reservations?

You reserve seats separately for each trip, using the pass.

Is it worth it for one Seoul–Busan return?

Usually not. It pays off with several long trips in a few days.

Did the SRT merger change the pass?

SRT was folded into KORAIL on 1 Sep 2026. Check that your route is covered before you buy.
