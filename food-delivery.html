Check your own setup
Step by step, first time
  1. Install Baemin (배달의민족) and switch the language to English in the app's settings.
  2. Sign up with your home phone number: pick your country code and enter the code sent by SMS. Your phone must be able to receive SMS while roaming.
  3. Set the delivery address. Paste the Korean road-name address, then add the building, floor, room number and any door code in the detail field.
  4. Pick a restaurant. The minimum order and the delivery fee are shown on the restaurant's page before you order.
  5. If a menu is only in Korean, point Papago's camera at the screen to translate it.
  6. Pay in the app with a foreign card, or with Apple Pay on iPhone. Add a note for the rider, such as "Please leave it at the door" (문 앞에 두고 가 주세요).
  7. Track the rider in the app. Many riders leave the order at the door and send a photo.
Common problems and fixes

  • The SMS code never arrives.

    Check the country code, wait a minute before asking again, and make sure your plan receives SMS abroad. If it still fails, order as a guest or ask your host.

  • Your card is declined.

    Try another card or Apple Pay. Some banks block a first payment to a Korean shop until you approve it in your bank's app.

  • The app can't find your address.

    Use the road-name address (a road name plus a building number), not a description of the place.

  • The rider can't get into the building.

    Put the door code and floor in the note, or meet the rider at the entrance. Your host may have a usual pickup spot.

Questions people ask

Can I order to a hotel or a park?

Hotels yes: add your room number, and the front desk may receive it. Seoul has delivery zones in Han River parks.

Are there other delivery apps?

Coupang Eats and Yogiyo are popular but usually need a Korean number to sign up. Shuttle is an English delivery service many foreigners use.

Do I tip the rider?

No. Tipping is not expected. The delivery fee is shown before you pay.

Is there a minimum order?

Most restaurants set one, shown on their page. Eating alone? Look for single-serving menus, marked 1인분.
