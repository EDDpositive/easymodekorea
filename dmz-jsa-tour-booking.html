How do I book a DMZ or JSA tour in Korea?

Book a guided tour in advance and carry your physical passport on the day. JSA visits can't be arranged on the spot.

Step by step, first time

  1. Book a DMZ or JSA tour ahead through a tour operator or a travel site.

    Same-day JSA booking is generally not possible, because names are registered in advance.

  2. Enter your passport details exactly as printed when you book.

    The list is checked against your passport on the day.

  3. Bring the physical passport on the tour day.

    Copies and expired passports are not accepted, and operators often do not refund a missed entry.

Step by step, first time
  1. Decide what you want to see. DMZ tours usually cover Imjingak, a tunnel and an observatory. The Joint Security Area (JSA) is a separate, stricter tour.
  2. Book with a tour operator or travel site well ahead. JSA lists are registered in advance, so same-day booking is generally not possible.
  3. Enter your name and passport number exactly as printed. The list is checked against your passport on the day.
  4. Read the operator's terms: some ask for a passport copy days ahead, some set a minimum age or a dress code, and some JSA tours exclude certain passports.
  5. On the day, bring your physical passport. Copies and expired passports are not accepted.
  6. Expect changes: visits can be cancelled at short notice for security reasons. Ask the operator how refunds work.
Common problems and fixes

  • You forgot your passport.

    You won't be let in, and operators often don't refund a missed entry. Go back for it if there is time.

  • The tour was cancelled at short notice.

    Ask the operator for a refund or another date. Security closures are outside their control.

  • No JSA slots are left.

    Book a regular DMZ tour instead, or visit Imjingak Park, which is open without a booking.

  • The name on your booking doesn't match your passport.

    Contact the operator straight away to correct it before the list is sent.

Questions people ask

Can I visit the DMZ on my own?

Only some areas such as Imjingak Park. Most DMZ sites and the JSA need a booked tour.

How far ahead should I book the JSA?

As early as possible; slots are limited and names must be registered in advance.

Is there an official 2026 rulebook?

We found operator and local-government pages but no single official 2026 notice, so follow your operator's instructions.

Do I need cash on the tour?

Bring a little for snacks and souvenirs; tour fees are paid when you book.
