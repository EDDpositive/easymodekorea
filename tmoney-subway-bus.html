Step by step, first time
  1. Buy a T-money card at a convenience store (CU, GS25, 7-Eleven, emart24) or a station machine. The card costs a few thousand won and the card price is not refundable.
  2. Top up with cash: give the card and cash to the convenience store staff and say "T-money charge, please", or use the top-up machine in any subway station. Station machines take cash.
  3. Subway: tap the card on the gate reader going in, and again going out.
  4. Bus: tap when you get on, and tap again on the reader by the rear door when you get off. Tapping off is what gives you the transfer discount on your next ride.
  5. Transfers between subway and bus are discounted when you tap the next ride soon after getting off (about 30 minutes in Seoul, longer late at night).
  6. The same card works on subways and buses in most Korean cities, and in many taxis and convenience stores.
  7. iPhone: Mobile T-money can now top up with some foreign cards. Try it on day one and keep the physical card as a backup.
  8. Leaving Korea: small balances can be refunded at convenience stores for a small fee (500 won at our last check). Or keep the card for your next trip.
Common problems and fixes

  • The gate beeps and won't open.

    Your balance is too low. Top up at the machine next to the gates.

  • The gate or bus reader doesn't read the card.

    Take the card out of your wallet or phone case so it isn't read together with another card, and hold it on the reader for a second.

  • The station machine won't take your foreign card.

    Top-up machines are made for cash. Use cash at the machine or a convenience store counter, or try Mobile T-money on iPhone.

  • You didn't get the transfer discount.

    You probably didn't tap off the bus, or took too long between rides. Tap off every time.

Questions people ask

How much should I top up?

A Seoul subway ride starts at 1,550 won by card (the base fare since June 2025). 10,000 to 20,000 won lasts a few days for most visitors; add more when the balance gets low.

Does T-money work outside Seoul?

Yes. It works on subways and buses in most cities, including Busan, Daegu and Jeju.

Can two people share one card?

Use one card per person. Each rider needs their own tap at the subway gates.

Is a tourist pass better than T-money?

Only if you ride a lot inside Seoul. See our Seoul tourist transit pass guide to compare.
