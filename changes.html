What changed in 2026

  1. 2026-10-01KTX tickets now open two months ahead.
  2. 2026-09-01SRT was folded into KORAIL. All high-speed trains are now sold as KTX.
  3. 2026-09-01The 30-day Climate Card was replaced by K-Pass. The 1 to 7-day tourist passes are still sold.
  4. 2026-08The KORAIL+ app replaced Korail Talk.
  5. 2026-07Baemin: visitors can order with a foreign phone number. Seoul added delivery zones at Han River parks.
  6. 2026-06-04Naver added passport verification, so visitors can book and pay on Naver Map.
  7. 2026-06-02Baemin: Apple Pay now accepts foreign cards.
  8. 2026-03Mobile T-money on iPhone started accepting some foreign cards for top-up.
  9. 2026-02-04Baemin added English, Japanese and Chinese.
  10. 2026-02The government conditionally approved Google's map-data export. Better Google routing is not confirmed yet.
  11. 2026-01-01Foreign visitors, including those exempt from K-ETA, must submit the e-Arrival Card within 72 hours before arrival.
