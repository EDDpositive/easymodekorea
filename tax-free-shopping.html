Step by step, first time
  1. Shop where you see a TAX FREE or TAX REFUND sign, and carry your actual passport. A photo of it is usually not accepted.
  2. Spend at least 15,000 won in one payment at one store. Receipts from separate payments can't be added together.
  3. Ask for an instant refund at the till. For payments under 1 million won, the tax can come off straight away, up to 5 million won in total per trip.
  4. No instant refund? Keep the refund receipt the store gives you, and claim it at a city refund kiosk or at the airport kiosk before you check in your bags.
  5. Keep the goods unused and with you until you leave. Customs may ask to see them, especially for large refunds.
  6. Claim within 3 months of purchase. Unclaimed refund receipts expire.
Common problems and fixes

  • The store says it can't do an instant refund.

    Take the refund receipt and claim it at a refund kiosk in the city or at the airport.

  • Your purchase was just under 15,000 won.

    Add an item and pay again in one payment, or buy it without a refund.

  • The airport refund queue is long.

    Arrive early, or claim at a downtown kiosk before your departure day.

  • You forgot your passport.

    Most stores need the real passport for the refund. Pay later with it, or keep the receipt and ask the store whether it can still issue a refund receipt.

Questions people ask

Do cosmetics count?

Cosmetic products bought in shops do. Cosmetic and skin clinic treatments no longer qualify from 2026.

Can residents with an ARC claim?

Usually not. Tourist refunds are for visitors staying under 6 months.

How much do I get back?

Most of the 10% tax, minus a service fee that depends on the refund company and the amount.

Can cruise passengers claim?

Yes. International cruise passengers became eligible for instant and downtown refunds in April 2026.
