Is the Seoul Climate Card tourist pass worth buying?

One extra step

The 1, 2, 3, 5 and 7-day Climate Card tourist passes are still sold. Only the 30-day card ended, in September 2026. Check the price and where to buy before you go.

Step by step, first time
  1. Count your rides. Add up the subway and bus trips you plan inside Seoul each day.
  2. Compare: a Seoul subway ride starts at 1,550 won by card (the base fare since June 2025). A pass only pays off on days with many rides.
  3. If it pays off, buy a 1, 2, 3, 5 or 7-day Climate Card tourist pass. Reports name station kiosks, tourist information centres and T-Money Town near Seoul Station as places to buy it.
  4. Check the current price, the card fee and how the days are counted when you buy.
  5. Use it on Seoul subways and buses. Keep a T-money card for taxis, the AREX Express and trips outside the Seoul area.
  6. The 30-day Climate Card ended in September 2026, and visitors on a tourist stay can't join K-Pass, so ignore guides that suggest either.
Common problems and fixes

  • The gate rejects the pass outside Seoul.

    Tourist passes work only in the Seoul area. Use T-money there.

  • The kiosk won't take your card.

    Pay with cash or buy at a staffed counter. Reports say some kiosks accept foreign cards since March 2026, with a fee.

  • The pass expired sooner than you expected.

    Days may be counted by calendar day, not 24-hour blocks. Check the rule when you buy.

  • You can't find where to buy one.

    Ask at a tourist information centre or a station office.

Questions people ask

Is the Climate Card still on sale for tourists?

Yes. The 1 to 7-day tourist passes continue; only the 30-day card ended.

Is a tourist pass cheaper than T-money?

Only on days with many rides. For a few rides a day, T-money is usually cheaper.

Does it work on the airport train?

Not on the AREX Express. Check the pass terms for the all-stop train.

Can visitors use K-Pass?

No. K-Pass is for residents; visitors on a tourist stay can't register.
