Do I need K-ETA to visit Korea?

One extra step

One extra step

Many passports are exempt from K-ETA through 31 Dec 2026, but not all. Check your own country on the official site before you fly.

Where
Official sites only: k-eta.go.kr and e-arrivalcard.go.kr ✓ 20 Nov 2026
Cost
K-ETA 10,000 won. The e-Arrival Card is free. ✓ 20 Nov 2026
Time
e-Arrival Card: within 72 hours before you land ✓ 20 Nov 2026

Checked against K-ETA (official site) on 20 Nov 2026

Step by step, first time

  1. Check your passport country on the official K-ETA site (k-eta.go.kr). The temporary exemption for eligible countries runs from 1 Jan to 31 Dec 2026.
  2. If you are not exempt, apply for K-ETA before you fly, not at check-in.
  3. Since 1 Jan 2026, foreign visitors, including those exempt from K-ETA, must submit the e-Arrival Card online within 72 hours before arrival. If you already hold an approved K-ETA, you don't need it.

Check your own setup
Step by step, first time
  1. Check your passport country on the official K-ETA site, www.k-eta.go.kr. Some countries are exempt until 31 Dec 2026; the rest need a K-ETA.
  2. If you need a K-ETA, apply only on the official site or the official K-ETA app. The fee is 10,000 won per person. The government has not appointed any agents, and agent sites charge more.
  3. Apply at least 72 hours before you board; a week or more ahead is safer. Have your passport, an email address, a recent face photo and your trip details ready.
  4. An approved K-ETA is valid for 3 years, or until your passport expires if that is sooner, and covers repeat trips. With it, you don't need the e-Arrival Card.
  5. Exempt from K-ETA? Submit the e-Arrival Card on the official site, www.e-arrivalcard.go.kr, within the 72 hours before you arrive.
  6. Have your address in Korea ready, because both forms ask where you will stay. Copy it from your booking. If your host uses our host page, the link they send has the address in English and Korean.
  7. Keep the confirmation on your phone, and carry the passport you applied with.
Common problems and fixes

  • A site asks for much more than 10,000 won.

    You are on an agent site. Close it and apply at www.k-eta.go.kr.

  • Your K-ETA is refused.

    You can't enter visa-free on that passport. Apply for a visa at a Korean embassy or consulate.

  • You made a mistake in your name or passport number.

    Submit a new application with the details exactly as printed in your passport.

  • Your flight was delayed past the 72 hours.

    An e-Arrival Card is no longer valid if you don't arrive within 72 hours. Submit a new one.

Questions people ask

My country is exempt. Do I still need anything?

Yes: the e-Arrival Card. You can also apply for a K-ETA voluntarily (the fee applies), and then you skip the e-Arrival Card.

How long does K-ETA approval take?

Often within 72 hours, but it can take longer, so apply a week or more before you fly.

Does each child need a K-ETA?

Yes. Each traveller applies separately, including children.

What happens in 2027?

The exemption runs to 31 Dec 2026. Rules for 2027 had not been announced at our last check; we will update this page.
