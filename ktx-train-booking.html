Step by step, first time
  1. Open KORAIL's English booking site (letskorail.com) or the KORAIL+ app. Search by departure station, arrival station and date.
  2. Pick the station carefully. Seoul trains leave from Seoul, Yongsan or Suseo stations. Since 1 Sep 2026, trains from Suseo are sold as KTX too.
  3. Choose a train and seat. Standard class is fine for most trips; first class has wider seats.
  4. Enter your name and passport details exactly as printed, then pay with your card.
  5. Save the ticket on your phone. It shows the car (호차) and seat number. There are no ticket gates; a conductor may check your ticket on board.
  6. Be on the platform a few minutes early, find your car number on the platform signs, and put large bags in the rack at the end of the car.
  7. Tickets open two months ahead (since 1 Oct 2026). For Friday, Sunday and holiday trains, book as soon as your dates are fixed.
Common problems and fixes

  • Your card fails on the website.

    Try the KORAIL+ app, another card, a reseller such as Klook, or a station ticket office.

  • Your train is sold out.

    Check earlier or later trains and Suseo or Yongsan departures. Standing tickets may be sold when seats run out.

  • You booked the wrong date or train.

    Cancel and rebook in the same channel. Fees rise as departure gets closer and are higher on weekends and holidays.

  • The train left without you.

    Go to a station ticket office straight away. Refunds after departure are only handled there.

Questions people ask

Do I still need to book SRT separately?

No. SRT was folded into KORAIL on 1 Sep 2026, and all high-speed trains are sold as KTX.

Can I book KTX without a Korean phone number?

Yes. The English website takes passport details and a foreign card.

Is the KR Pass worth it?

Only for several long trips in a few days. See our KR Pass guide to compare.

Is there Wi-Fi and food on board?

Plan to bring your own snacks and use your own data; services on board vary by train.
