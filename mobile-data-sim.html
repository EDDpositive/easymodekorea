Check your own setup
Step by step, first time
  1. Check that your phone is unlocked and supports eSIM. Most recent iPhones and many Android phones do.
  2. Decide what you need. Data only covers maps, messaging and visitor apps. Choose a plan with a Korean 010 number only if you want Korean SMS codes.
  3. Buy the eSIM before you fly and install it at home on Wi-Fi by scanning the QR code. Don't delete it later: most eSIMs can't be reinstalled.
  4. On landing, switch the eSIM line on, turn on data roaming for that line only, and set it as your data line. Keep your home line on for calls and bank SMS if you need them.
  5. Prefer a physical SIM or a pocket Wi-Fi? Carrier counters in the Incheon arrivals hall sell and rent them. Bring your passport.
  6. Know the limit: even a Korean 010 number usually fails full identity checks (본인인증) without a residence card. Use passport verification where a service offers it, such as Naver.
Common problems and fixes

  • The eSIM is installed but there's no data.

    Turn on data roaming for the eSIM line, and make it the line used for mobile data. Restart the phone if it still shows no signal.

  • A Korean app won't send its code to your number.

    Many Korean apps only text Korean numbers. Use the visitor version of the app (k.ride, CatchTable Global) or a passport sign-up instead.

  • Identity check (본인인증) fails with your Korean SIM.

    That check needs a number registered to a residence card. Look for a passport or email option, or ask your host for help.

  • You deleted the eSIM by mistake.

    Contact the eSIM seller. Many can issue a new QR code; some can't.

Questions people ask

eSIM, physical SIM or pocket Wi-Fi?

eSIM is easiest if your phone supports it. A pocket Wi-Fi suits groups sharing one connection. A physical SIM suits phones without eSIM.

Do I need a Korean phone number?

Not for most visitors. Maps, taxis, delivery and restaurant bookings now have visitor routes without one.

Is there free Wi-Fi in Korea?

Yes, in many subway stations, cafés and public spaces, but don't rely on it for directions on the move.

Will my bank's SMS codes still reach me?

Only if your home line stays on and roaming lets you receive SMS. Check with your carrier before you fly.
