Taxi problem

What to do, in order

  1. Before you get out: ask for the receipt and note the plate number.

    A photo of both is enough.

  2. Is the fare right? In Seoul it starts at 4,800 won, plus 20% from 22:00 to 04:00 and 40% from 23:00 to 02:00. ✓ 20 Nov 2026

    Taxi

    Taxi
  3. Report overcharging or a refused ride: call 1330, the Tourist Complaint Center. ✓ 22 Nov 2026

    Since 24 Oct 2025 it is linked with the local 120 lines, and reports are passed to the local government.

    Asia Business Daily: 1330 and 120 complaint lines linked (28 Oct 2025)

    Call 1330
  4. In Seoul you can also call 120 and press 9 for English, Chinese, Japanese, Vietnamese or Mongolian, weekdays 9:00 to 18:00. ✓ 22 Nov 2026

    Guro-gu office, Seoul: 120 Dasan foreign-language service

    Call 02-120
  5. Seoul has run a QR-code system for reporting unfair taxi practices since June 2025. ✓ 22 Nov 2026

    Korea Times: QR reports for Seoul taxis (20 Jan 2026)

  6. Rode with k.ride or Uber? Report it in the app's ride history.

    The route and fare are already recorded there.

  7. Feel unsafe? Ask to stop, get out where there are people, and call 112.

    Call 112
