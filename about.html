About EasyModeKorea

Who runs EasyModeKorea, how each answer is checked and dated, and how to report a mistake.

What this is

EasyModeKorea is an independent guide for foreign visitors to Korea. It answers one question per topic: does this Korean app or service work with my phone number, my phone and my cards, and what do I do if it doesn't?

How we check

Each answer is checked against official notices (government, operators and the app makers) and recent news reports, and shows the date we last checked it. When sources disagree, we say so and give a backup route. We don't publish prices or rules we can't trace to a source.

Independence

We are not affiliated with any app, company or government body named on this site. Rules change often, so confirm in the app before you rely on an answer.

Mistakes and updates

If something here is wrong or out of date, tell us and we will fix the page and note the change on the What changed page.
