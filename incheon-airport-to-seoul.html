How do I get from Incheon Airport to Seoul?

Good to go

Good to go

Every option works for visitors. The AREX train is the simplest.

Where
AREX train from the arrivals floor; follow the Train signs ✓ 17 Nov 2026
Time
AREX Express: about 43 min (T1) or 51 min (T2) to Seoul Station ✓ 17 Nov 2026
Cost

Step by step, first time

  1. AREX Express: non-stop to Seoul Station, about 43 minutes from Terminal 1 and 51 from Terminal 2. Buy the ticket at the machine or counter.
  2. AREX all-stop train: about an hour, stops at Hongik Univ. and Gongdeok. Tap a T-money card.
  3. Airport bus: goes to major hotels and areas. Ask at the bus desk in arrivals for your route number.
  4. Taxi: k.ride or Uber, or the taxi stand outside arrivals.

If that failsTrains don't run all night. After the last train, take a taxi or a late-night airport bus.

Step by step, first time
  1. After customs, follow the signs for Trains (Airport Railroad, AREX). The station is inside the airport, under the Transportation Center.
  2. For Seoul Station with no stops, buy an AREX Express ticket at the machine or the AREX counter. It takes about 43 minutes from Terminal 1 and 51 from Terminal 2.
  3. For Hongik Univ., Gongdeok or Digital Media City, take the all-stop train instead. Tap a T-money card at the gate; it is much cheaper than the Express and takes about an hour to Seoul Station.
  4. No T-money yet? Convenience stores in the arrivals hall sell cards. Top up with cash at the counter.
  5. Airport buses go straight to many hotel areas. Ask at the bus information desk in arrivals for the route number to your area, then buy a ticket at the desk or kiosk.
  6. Taxi: use the taxi stand outside arrivals, or call k.ride or Uber. It is the easiest choice with a lot of luggage, and the most expensive.
  7. Arriving late at night: trains stop around midnight. Check the last train on arex.or.kr, and plan on a late-night airport bus or a taxi after that.
Common problems and fixes

  • You can't find the train station.

    Look for signs saying Train or Airport Railroad, or ask any information desk for AREX.

  • The Express ticket machine rejects your card.

    Buy at the staffed AREX counter, or take the all-stop train with T-money.

  • The gate won't open on the all-stop train.

    Your T-money balance is too low. Top up at the machine before the gates.

  • You missed the last train.

    Take a late-night airport bus or a taxi. Ask the bus desk which routes still run.

Questions people ask

Express or all-stop train?

Express if you are going to Seoul Station and want a seat with no stops. All-stop if your hotel is near Hongik Univ. or Gongdeok, or to save money.

How much is the AREX Express?

Fares changed in 2026 and sources disagree, so we don't quote one. Check arex.or.kr or the counter.

Can I use T-money on the Express?

The Express needs its own ticket. T-money works on the all-stop train.

Is Gimpo Airport different?

Yes. Gimpo is closer to Seoul and on subway lines 5 and 9 as well as AREX. This guide covers Incheon.
