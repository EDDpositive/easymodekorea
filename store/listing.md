## English (default)

**App name** (27): `EasyModeKorea: Korea Travel`

**Short description** (78): `Short answers for visitors to Korea, checked and dated. Korean cards. Offline.`

**Full description**

```
Korea runs on apps, and many of them ask for a Korean phone number. EasyModeKorea tells you what works for a visitor, in a few lines, with the date each answer was last checked.

WHAT YOU GET
• Short answers first: the verdict, where, cost and time, then numbered steps.
• Checked and dated: every key fact shows the date it was checked and the source. Each guide lists what changed this year, the part older guides and AI answers get wrong.
• Answers for your phone: tell the app once whether you have a Korean number, and it shows what works for you.
• Korean cards: show a driver or staff a sentence in big Korean letters, or copy place names into a map app. Make your own card for a hotel name or an allergy.
• Places to go: save your stay and other places once, show them to a taxi driver full screen, or open them in Naver Map or KakaoMap.
• Food and payment cards: allergies, vegetarian, no pork, or a card that won't go through, in big Korean.
• Emergency help: the numbers to call and Korean sentences to show, one tap away.
• Arrival helper: enter your landing time and see when you can submit the e-Arrival Card.
• Works offline: guides are stored on your phone, so they open on the plane or underground.
• Save guides for your trip, and search every guide, question and Korean card.

TOPICS
Entry rules and arrival forms, airport to the city, subway and bus, transit cards and passes, maps, taxis, KTX trains, mobile data and eSIM, cash and ATMs, restaurant bookings, food delivery, translation, tax-free shopping, luggage storage, pharmacies and hospitals, power plugs, Busan, Jeju and the DMZ.

FIVE LANGUAGES
English, Japanese, Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese and Vietnamese.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads. Your saved guides, address and cards stay on your phone.

INDEPENDENT
EasyModeKorea is an independent guide. It is not affiliated with any app, company or government body it writes about. Rules change often, so confirm in the official app or site before you rely on an answer.

Questions or corrections: edandco.biz@gmail.com
```

### Data safety

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Does the app collect or share any required user data types? | Yes |
| Is all collected data encrypted in transit? | Yes (HTTPS only) |
| Can users request that data be deleted? | Yes. By email; method described at https://easymodekorea.com/privacy.html |

| Data type | Collected | Shared | Optional? | Purpose | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| App activity: App interactions | Yes | No | Required | Analytics | Page views and button taps are counted with GoatCounter (no cookies, no IP addresses stored, no user profile). |
| Location: Approximate location | Yes | No | Required | Analytics | The analytics service keeps the visitor's country, worked out from the IP address. Declared to be safe. It can be switched off in GoatCounter's settings; then answer No. |
| Personal info: Email address | Yes | No | Optional | App functionality | Only if the user types it into the question or feedback form, to get a reply. |
| App activity: Other user-generated content | Yes | No | Optional | App functionality | The text of a question or feedback the user sends. |

Not collected: name, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, files, payment info, device IDs. The stay address, saved guides and Korean cards never leave the phone, so they are not "collected".

"Shared" stays No: GoatCounter, Google Forms and the hosts only process data for the site, which Google does not count as sharing.
