Privacy policy

In short

EasyModeKorea has no accounts, no ads and no tracking cookies. The Android app shows this website in full screen. It does not ask for access to your location, contacts, camera or files.

What stays on your phone

Your phone setup, your Korea Ready check answers, trip dates, saved guides and places, checklist ticks, your stay's address and the Korean cards you make are stored only in the browser or app on your phone. They are never sent to us. Erase them on the My trip page, or by clearing the site's data in your browser. If you make a share link for a companion, your stay address and places are written inside that link and nowhere else, so anyone who gets the link can read them.

Visit counts

We count page views and button taps with GoatCounter. It sets no cookies and stores no IP addresses. It shows us totals: which pages were opened, the referring site, browser and system type, screen width, language and country. We cannot tell who you are. If you open a link your host made for you, the name of the stay in that link is counted too.

Questions and feedback

When you send a question or feedback, your message, the page you sent it from and your email address (only if you enter one) go to a Google Form that we own. We use them to answer you and to fix the guides. Email us to have a message deleted.

Other companies' servers

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so those servers receive your IP address and browser type when a page loads, as any web server does. If you tap Naver Map or KakaoMap on a saved address or place, that text is sent to that map service as a search. Outside links lead to services with their own policies.

Children

EasyModeKorea is written for adult travelers and is not directed at children under 13.

Contact and changes

Questions or deletion requests: edandco.biz@gmail.com. When this policy changes, the date below changes.
